<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am currently a Research Fellow with the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, working in the [Automated Driving and Human-Machine System (AutoMan) Lab](https://lvchen.wixsite.com/automan). I received my Ph.D. from the School of Vehicle and Mobility at Tsinghua University, and my B.S. degree in Mechanical Design and Automation from Zhejiang University.

My research lies at the intersection of autonomous robotic systems, physical AI, and intelligent control. I develop algorithms and systems that enable mobile robots and intelligent vehicles to perceive and model complex environments, reason over long horizons, and execute reliable actions under uncertainty. My current work focuses on building resilient intelligent embodied systems for single and swarm robots to navigate, explore, and understand challenging unknown environments. I am also interested in robust data-driven control for autonomous vehicles and intelligent chassis systems.

    <p><strong>APEX: Goal-Directed Navigation in Unknown Multi-Layer Environments</strong></p>
    <p>APEX reformulates navigation to an unseen goal as a structured exploration problem. The system combines traversability graph mapping, decision-level graph reasoning, frontier evaluation, and receding-horizon route selection so that the robot explores only the regions that matter for reaching the target. The framework has been validated in both simulation and real-world multi-layer environments, including office-scale spaces and parking garages.</p>

- **Data-Driven Predictive Vehicle Control**: signal-based system representation and model-free predictive control for autonomous vehicle motion control.
